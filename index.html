Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know before you get started.

Is this planner suitable for beginners?

Yes. Level 1 is built for beginners and low-energy days, and you move up only when you feel ready.

Do I need special equipment?

No. You need a sturdy chair, a wall, and comfortable shoes. Light weights are optional.

Can I still do this if I have joint pain?

The movements are low-impact and joint-friendly, but check with your doctor first and stop if you feel sharp pain.

How soon will I see results?

Everyone is different and no planner can promise results. This one helps you build simple habits and track your progress.

Is this a diet plan?

No. It is a gentle habit system with a simple plate guide and an optional eating-window tracker. It is not a diet or medical nutrition plan.

What if I miss a day?

Restart at your next meal or movement. Missing a day does not erase your progress.

Can I use this with my medication?

Talk with your doctor first, especially before changing meal timing if you take medication that affects blood sugar or blood pressure.
